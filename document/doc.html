VigilDrive AI
Multi-Modal Driver Safety, Crash Telemetry & Automated Emergency Dispatch Ecosystem
Domain: Embedded IoT, Computer Vision, Cloud APIs & Mobile Systems
Target Audience: Two-Wheelers & Three-Wheelers (Fleet / Commuter)
Hardware Prototype: ESP32 Microcontroller + MPU-6050 6-Axis IMU
Software Stack: Python (FastAPI), Flutter Mobile, MediaPipe, OpenCV

1. Executive Summary & Problem Statement

Road traffic collisions involving two-wheelers and three-wheelers represent the highest percentage of vehicular fatalities across developing nations. Critical factors contributing to high mortality include delayed emergency response (the Golden Hour dilemma) and driver fatigue/distraction. Existing commercial smart helmets are prohibitively expensive ($150–$400), non-transferable, and require specialized hardware configurations.

VigilDrive AI introduces an ultra-low-cost, multi-modal safety ecosystem. It couples a universal clip-on IoT module for any existing motorcycle helmet with an edge-vision cabin fatigue monitoring system for three-wheelers, offloading high-cost cellular and GPS computation to the rider's smartphone.

3. Key Technical Novelty & Engineering Highlights

  • Edge-Offloaded Cost Optimization: By routing GPS acquisition, high-speed cellular transmission, and SMS dispatching through the rider's mobile device via BLE, the hardware production cost is maintained below LKR 4,500 (~$15) with extended multi-day battery endurance.
  • Dual-Stage Crash Verification (Anti-False Alarm): Sudden impact threshold spikes (>4G) initiate a local audio alert and a 10-second mobile countdown. If the rider cancels the prompt, false SOS alerts from accidental helmet drops are completely eliminated.
  • Dual-Mode Hybrid Architecture: Provides two-wheeler safety via IMU-based kinematics (impact + head nodding) and three-wheeler cabin safety via non-intrusive facial landmark tracking.

6. Supervision & Research Objectives

  • Kinematic Calibration: Refining accelerometer threshold limits and Kalman filtering algorithms to isolate actual crash profiles from road vibrations and speed bumps.
  • Mobile Inference Optimization: Optimizing edge-vision frame throughput to achieve high accuracy without device thermal throttling.
  • Formal Evaluation: Establishing verified metrics on response latency, battery longevity, and crash detection accuracy for academic publication and incubation pitches.
