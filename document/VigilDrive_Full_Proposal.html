PROJECT PROPOSAL: VIGILDRIVE AI
Multi-Modal Driver Safety Ecosystem via Context-Aware Telemetry, Embedded Kinematics & Edge Vision
Project Domain: Embedded IoT, Computer Vision, Sensor Fusion & Telemetry
Target Scope: Two-Wheeler Commuters & Three-Wheeler / Light Fleets
Hardware Prototype: ESP32 SoC + MPU-6050 (6-DOF IMU) via BLE 5.0
Software Architecture: Flutter Mobile OS Gateway + FastAPI Microservices

1. Executive Summary & Problem Formulation

Road traffic collisions involving two-wheelers and three-wheelers represent over 50% of vehicular fatalities in developing transit infrastructures. The major determinants of mortality are the Golden Hour emergency medical service (EMS) dispatch delay and undetected driver microsleep/distraction.

While high-end commercial smart helmets exist, their prohibitive cost ($150–$400) and vehicle-locked architectures prevent widespread adoption. VigilDrive AI solves this through an Edge-Offloaded Paradigm: a universal, ultra-low-cost (~$15 / LKR 4,500) clip-on IoT module attached to any standard helmet coupled with a smartphone gateway that handles intensive compute, live satellite GPS indexing, and direct GSM emergency dispatching.

4. Mathematical Formulations & Detection Logic

4.1. Total G-Force Vector Magnitude (Impact Detection)

Calculates the instantaneous linear resultant acceleration across all three spatial axes:

|A_total| = sqrt(ax^2 + ay^2 + az^2) / 9.80665 [Trigger Condition: |A_total| >= 4.5G]

4.2. Angular Pitch & Nodding Computation (IMU Kinematics)

Extracts gravitational tilt vectors to identify forward head drooping during fatigue:

Pitch_tilt = atan2(-ax, sqrt(ay^2 + az^2)) * (180 / PI) [Trigger Condition: Pitch > 25 deg for >= 1.5s]

4.3. Eye Aspect Ratio - EAR (Computer Vision Pipeline)

Quantifies eye openness using 6 facial landmark Euclidean coordinates per eye:

EAR = (||p2 - p6|| + ||p3 - p5||) / (2 * ||p1 - p4||) [Trigger Condition: EAR < 0.22 for >= 1.5s]

5. Multi-Sensor Context Validation Matrix

To prevent false positives, inertial spikes from the helmet are evaluated against kinematic data from the smartphone's GPS:

Normal Dynamic Cruising Low Vibration (|A| < 1.5G) Velocity > 25 km/h, Heading Stable Normal Transit Passive Telemetry Log
Accidental Helmet Drop High Impact Spike (|A| > 4.5G) Velocity = 0 km/h (Static Vehicle) False Alarm (Static Drop) Suppressed (No SOS Dispatch)

8. Research & Supervision Objectives

  • Dynamic Filter Tuning: Implementation of a Kalman filter to eliminate high-frequency engine vibration noise from the IMU signal.
  • Edge-Vision Frame Optimization: Limiting optical processing to 6–8 FPS at 320x240 resolution to eliminate device thermal throttling.
  • Empirical Benchmarking: Measuring crash classification accuracy, false-positive suppression rate, and end-to-end SMS dispatch latency for conference publication and startup grant applications.
